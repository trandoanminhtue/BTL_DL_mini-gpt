\begin{abstract}
We implement a 10--30 million parameter GPT-style language model from scratch and train it on Vietnamese text. Interpolated n-gram and LSTM models serve as baselines under a shared BPE tokenizer. We compare perplexity and sampled continuations, plot validation loss against parameter count, and study temperature, top-$k$, and top-$p$ decoding. Light instruction tuning is optional. All reported numbers must be copied from training logs; placeholders below are left blank until checkpoints exist.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

Language modeling is the task of assigning probability to token sequences~\cite{jurafsky2023slp}. Decoder-only Transformers~\cite{vaswani2017attention,radford2018improving,radford2019language} are the standard architecture. This paper asks whether a \emph{small} GPT, written without relying on \texttt{transformers.GPT2LMHeadModel}, can beat classical baselines on Vietnamese and whether loss decreases as we scale from $\sim$7M to $\sim$27M parameters~\cite{kaplan2020scaling}.

\section{Related work}

Neural LMs~\cite{bengio2003neural}, RNN LMs~\cite{mikolov2010recurrent}, LSTM~\cite{hochreiter1997lstm}, BPE~\cite{sennrich2016neural}, GPT~\cite{radford2018improving,radford2019language}, scaling laws~\cite{kaplan2020scaling}, and nucleus sampling~\cite{holtzman2020curious} frame the project. We do not claim SOTA; the contribution is a transparent student-scale study with released code.
